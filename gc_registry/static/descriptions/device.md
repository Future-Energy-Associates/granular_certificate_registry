Registration and management of production, consumption, or storage units against
which GC Bundles can be issued or cancelled. Each Device is associated with an
Account and specifies attributes such as energy source, technology type, operational
date, capacity, location, and grid connection.

Storage Devices are flagged separately and participate in the Storage workflow for
managing charge and discharge records and the issuance of SD-GC Bundles. Device
creation requires Production User privileges or above, and users can only manage
Devices associated with Accounts they have access to.
